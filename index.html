Who we are

GRITMAX is a London consultancy of software architects and senior engineers. Small enough that the people you meet in discovery are the people who design and build.

Our background is backend engineering for financial, crypto and e‑commerce products: trading and market data infrastructure, pricing services, payment and ledger flows, order and inventory systems. We have built them on most of the mainstream stacks, and we are deliberately unattached to any of them. The right technology is the one your team can still run with confidence in three years.

Much of our work is technical upgrade: platforms that have outgrown their original design and need to change while still serving customers. We plan those migrations so releases keep shipping while the foundations move underneath.

We do not sell teams by the dozen. Each engagement is a small group of senior people who write the code they design and document it for the team that inherits it.
